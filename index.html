First: sanitize

Before any data touches a public AI tool.

  • 01Remove names, emails, student IDs, and addresses.
  • 02Aggregate where you can — counts and summaries, not rows.
  • 03Never paste FERPA / PII into public chat tools.
  • 04Export a sanitized copy — work from that, keep raw files offline.
  • 05Re-sanitize each cycle; don't reuse last year's export blindly.
  • 06Brand colors & fonts from your style guide are fine — student photos are not.
